Background Tagger

How to use: open this page on your live site (/tools/tagger.html) or locally. Thumbnails load from CodePen in your browser. Tick the collections that match each background (a background may be in several), optionally fix the title, then click Download backgrounds.json and replace data/backgrounds.json in the repo. Edits are auto-saved in this browser until you download. Click a thumbnail to see the live pen.
