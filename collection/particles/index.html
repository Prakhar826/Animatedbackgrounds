Particle systems, snow, glitter, dots and flow fields. Popular searches: particle backgrounds, particle animation, snow and glitter backgrounds.

Particle systems give you depth without an image: dust, snow, glitter, fireflies or connected dots drifting across a dark field — a timeless motion background.

The Canvas implementations here use a single rAF loop and a fixed particle budget, so they stay smooth on laptops and phones alike.

Particles — frequently asked questions

How many particles should I use?

Between 60 and 120 on a typical hero section. Above roughly 250 the effect stops looking better and starts costing frames.

Can I make a snow background?

Yes — the winter entries in this collection are snow systems with adjustable drift and fall speed.
