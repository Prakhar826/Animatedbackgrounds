Noise & Glitch — frequently asked questions

How do I add film grain in CSS?

Overlay a tiled noise image (or an SVG turbulence filter) with mix-blend-mode: overlay and a low opacity — around 0.05 to 0.12.

Will glitch effects hurt accessibility?

Fast flashing can be a problem. Keep the cycle slow and respect prefers-reduced-motion.
