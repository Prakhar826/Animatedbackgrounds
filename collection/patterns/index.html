Patterns & Geometry — frequently asked questions

How do I make a repeating CSS pattern?

Use repeating-linear-gradient or repeating-conic-gradient on a pseudo-element, then animate background-position for motion.

Will patterns make text hard to read?

Use a low-contrast pattern and put a solid or blurred panel behind the text. Every card page shows the intended composition.
