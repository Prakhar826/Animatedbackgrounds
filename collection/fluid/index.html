Fluid Simulation — free animated backgrounds

Liquid, smoke and fluid-dynamics effects. Popular searches: fluid backgrounds, liquid backgrounds, smoke animation backgrounds.

Liquid, smoke and fluid-dynamics effects are the most organic background effects on the site — they feel alive because no two frames are identical.

Most are shader- or Canvas-based and use a low-resolution buffer upscaled with smoothing, which keeps them fast despite the heavy maths.

Fluid Simulation — frequently asked questions

What is a fluid simulation background?

A velocity field is advected and diffused each frame, so colour is carried around the canvas like ink in water.

Are these GPU heavy?

Yes — they are the most demanding family here. Use them on desktop hero sections rather than long pages.
