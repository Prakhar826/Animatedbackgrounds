Wave Animations — free animated backgrounds

Smooth sine waves, ocean motion and ripple effects. Popular searches: wave animation backgrounds, CSS wave backgrounds, ocean motion backgrounds.

Wave animations are the most versatile moving backgrounds on the web: they read as calm, premium and technical all at once, and because they are built from sine curves they loop perfectly forever.

Every wave here is a pure CSS or Canvas background animation — no video files, no plugins. Drop one behind a hero section, a pricing table or a full-screen landing page and it keeps adapting to any viewport size.

Wave Animations — frequently asked questions

How do I add a wave background to my site?

Open any background, press Embed to copy an iframe, or Get Code to copy the CSS and HTML into your own stylesheet. The step-by-step guide covers both routes.

Are these wave backgrounds free?

Yes. Every background links to its original CodePen, and most are MIT or free-to-use. Check the individual pen licence before commercial use.

Can I use a wave background in a dark theme?

Most of the collection is dark-by-default, and the light ones pair well with a translucent overlay. Try adding filter: brightness(.85) behind your content.
