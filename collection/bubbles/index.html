Bubbles, blobs and bokeh make an animation background feel soft and friendly — ideal for product pages, onboarding screens and anything aimed at consumers.

Morphing blobs are pure CSS border-radius animation, which means they cost almost nothing to render even on older phones.

Bubbles & Blobs — frequently asked questions

What is a blob background?

A blob is an organic shape animated by shifting its CSS border-radius over time. It reads as a soft floating object rather than a geometric box.

Do bubble backgrounds hurt performance?

Pure CSS ones do not — they animate on the compositor. Canvas versions use the GPU. Avoid stacking more than one animated layer per page.
