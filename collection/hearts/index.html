Hearts & Love — frequently asked questions

Are these heart backgrounds suitable for Valentine’s Day pages?

Yes — they were collected for exactly that. Pair a soft heart loop with a serif headline for a classic card look.

Can I change the heart colour?

Most CSS versions expose a single colour variable. Open the pen, change one hex value and every heart updates.
