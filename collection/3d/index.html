3D Backgrounds — frequently asked questions

Are WebGL backgrounds safe on mobile?

Yes, but cap the pixel ratio at 1.5 and pause rendering when the tab is hidden. The pens here already do both.

Which library do these use?

Most use Three.js or raw WebGL; a few are pure CSS 3D with transform-style: preserve-3d.
