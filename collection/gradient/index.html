Gradients — frequently asked questions

How do I make an animated gradient in CSS?

Set a linear-gradient with background-size: 400% 400%, then animate background-position from 0% 50% to 100% 50% and back. The Background Studio writes that CSS for you.

Which gradient is best for a hero section?

A two- or three-stop gradient at a low contrast ratio keeps text readable. Add a dark veil if your body copy sits on top.

Can I generate my own?

Yes — use the Background Studio to pick colours and speed, then copy the finished CSS.
